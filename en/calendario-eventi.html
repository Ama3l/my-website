The dust jacket consists of a front and back designed as an autonomous graphic object. The front features a dynamic, layered typographic composition in which keywords such as Art, Design & Cinema emerge from a fragmented layout built on visual and spatial contrasts. The interplay of positive and negative space, combined with an essential palette, gives the project a contemporary editorial quality. The back functions as an annual cultural calendar, visually distributing events, exhibitions and programmes across the months. Combining information and aesthetics turns it into a functional and narrative element that connects graphic design with real time. As a whole, the dust jacket is conceived as a printable object balancing design rigour and visual impact.
