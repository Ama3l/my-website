This video was created for the “Video for the Web” course, for which RSI Cult+ proposed a series of themes. I focused on a print shop well known among Visual Communication students for its constant support, both in urgent situations and through planned collaboration. The team has “saved” many students by printing projects on time and advising them on how to present their work effectively. The video therefore portrays the print shop and the relationship it has built with students over the years. The project was not selected by Cult+ because technical issues during filming required a change of direction.
