Möbius

Year

2024

Category

Visual identity, UI

Tools

Figma, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects

Course

Comunicazione Visiva, User Interface

In this project I created a new identity for Möbius in Lugano. The pictogram was developed from intersecting circular geometric forms. Connecting the lines while preserving the negative spaces generated the final shape. The visual communication for social media was also kept extremely simple, reflecting a clean and essential aesthetic.
