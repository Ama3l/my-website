The poster dedicated to Paul Schuitema features a bold typographic structure with strong visual impact. Its diagonal composition cuts decisively across the space, recalling Constructivist aesthetics and European modernism, particularly Dutch and Russian avant-garde design. The artist’s name, divided into two colour levels—black for “Paul” and intense red for “Schuitema”—dominates the surface and directs the viewer’s gaze. The geometric uppercase sans-serif typeface reinforces the rational and experimental identity of Schuitema’s work. Practical information such as dates, location and opening hours is integrated coherently without weakening the composition. The project balances visual dynamism with clear communication, paying tribute to Schuitema through both content and formal language.
